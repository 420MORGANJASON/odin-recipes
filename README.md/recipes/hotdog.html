Hotdog recipe

Prepare the grill: Preheat your grill to medium-high heat (around 400°F or 200°C). Grill the hot dogs: Place the hot dogs on the grill and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are heated through and slightly charred. Toast the buns (optional): If desired, lightly toast the hot dog buns on the grill for a few seconds per side.

Steps

  1. Prepare the grill: Preheat your grill to medium-high heat (around 400°F or 200°C).
  2. Grill the hot dogs: Place the hot dogs on the grill and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until they are heated through and slightly charred.
  3. Toast the buns (optional): If desired, lightly toast the hot dog buns on the grill for a few seconds per side.
